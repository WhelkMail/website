This Privacy Policy explains which personal data is processed when you visit
**whelkmail.eu** (the “Site”), why, and which rights you have.

## Data controller

The data controller is {{< privacy-controller >}}. For any question or
request about your personal data, contact {{< contact-email >}}.

## About this Site

The Site is a static marketing website for WhelkMail. You can browse it without
creating an account. We do not sell personal data, run advertising, or use
profiling or automated decision-making.

## Data we process

### Technical data and server logs

When you visit the Site, our hosting provider Cloudflare processes the technical
data needed to deliver and secure the Site: IP address, requested URL, date and
time, browser and operating system information, and security-related metadata.
This data is used to provide the Site, prevent abuse and keep it available.

Legal basis: our legitimate interest in operating a secure and reliable website
(Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Logs are kept only as long as necessary for security and
troubleshooting and are deleted or anonymised according to the hosting
provider’s retention schedule.

### Analytics (only with your consent)

If you accept analytics in the cookie banner, we load Google Tag Manager, which
in turn loads Google Analytics 4. Analytics helps us understand how visitors use
the Site (pages viewed, approximate location derived from the IP address,
device and browser, interactions) and improve it.

We do not load Google Tag Manager or Google Analytics before you consent. If you
reject analytics, no Google tag is loaded, no analytics cookie is set, and any
existing Google Analytics cookies for this Site are removed.

Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and Art. 122 of the Italian Privacy
Code, Legislative Decree 196/2003). You can withdraw your consent at any time
via “Cookie settings” in the footer.

### Google Search Console

We use Google Search Console to monitor how the Site appears in Google Search
and to check indexing. Site ownership can be verified with a DNS record or an
HTML meta tag; this verification does not set cookies. Search performance
reports are aggregated. If a verification tag is deployed through Google Tag
Manager, it is subject to the same consent gate as the rest of the container.

## Cookies and local storage

We use browser local storage to remember your cookie choice. Optional analytics
cookies are set only after consent. See the [Cookie Policy](/cookies/) for
names, purposes and durations.

## Recipients and international transfers

Personal data may be processed by:

- Cloudflare, Inc., which hosts the Site and processes technical data as our
  hosting provider;
- Google Ireland Limited and Google LLC, which provide Google Tag Manager,
  Google Analytics 4 and Google Search Console.

Google may process data outside the EEA, including in the United States.
Transfers are based on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (where the recipient is
certified) and/or the Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European
Commission. You can request more information using the contact details above.

## Retention

We keep personal data only for as long as necessary for the purposes described
above:

- analytics data: according to the retention setting of our Google Analytics 4
  property, and in any case no longer than 14 months;
- consent records: 6 months, after which we ask again;
- server logs: for the short period configured by our hosting provider for
  security and troubleshooting.

## Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access your personal data, rectify
inaccurate data, erase data, restrict or object to processing, receive your data
in a portable format, and withdraw consent at any time without affecting the
lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal.

To exercise these rights, contact {{< contact-email >}}. We will respond within
one month, extendable by two months where necessary.

## Complaints

If you believe your personal data is processed unlawfully, you can lodge a
complaint with the Italian data protection authority, the
{{< privacy-authority >}}, or with the supervisory authority in your country of
residence.

## Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes to the Site or applicable
law. The date at the top shows the latest revision.
