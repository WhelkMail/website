This Cookie Policy explains how **whelkmail.eu** uses cookies and similar
technologies, such as browser local storage.

## Your choice

On your first visit, a banner lets you accept or reject optional analytics. Both
choices are presented with equal prominence. We do not load Google Tag Manager
or Google Analytics before you make a choice.

You can change or withdraw your choice at any time using “Cookie settings” in
the footer. Rejecting analytics removes any Google Analytics cookies set for
this Site.

## Strictly necessary

These technologies are needed to remember your privacy choice and to keep the
Site secure. They do not require consent.

- **Local storage `whelkmail-consent`**: stores your analytics choice, a
  timestamp and the consent version. It stays in your browser for up to 6
  months, is not sent to our servers, and is used only to respect your choice.
- **Cloudflare security cookies** (for example `__cf_bm` or `cf_clearance`):
  if Cloudflare’s security features are active, they may set strictly necessary
  cookies to protect the Site from automated abuse. These cookies are managed by
  Cloudflare, Inc.

## Analytics (optional)

These cookies are set by Google Analytics 4 only after you accept analytics.
Google Tag Manager itself does not set cookies; it loads the tags allowed by
your choice.

| Cookie | Provider | Purpose | Duration |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `_ga` | Google Analytics | Distinguishes unique users | 2 years |
| `_ga_<container-id>` | Google Analytics | Persists session state | 2 years |

Analytics data is processed by Google Ireland Limited and Google LLC as
described in the [Privacy Policy](/privacy/). Google may transfer data outside
the EEA under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and/or the Standard Contractual
Clauses.

## Managing cookies

You can accept or reject analytics through “Cookie settings” in the footer. You
can also delete or block cookies in your browser settings; the Site continues to
work without analytics cookies. Blocking strictly necessary storage may cause
the consent banner to appear on every visit.
